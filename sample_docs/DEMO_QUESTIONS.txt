DOCUMIND AI — SAMPLE DEMO QUESTIONS
===================================

Option A — mixed-format citation demo:
Upload demo_campus_guide.pdf and demo_research_handbook.docx.

Option B — quick 4-document TXT demo:
Upload all four files named demo_*.txt from this folder:
  demo_campus_services.txt
  demo_student_handbook.txt
  demo_transport_notice.txt
  demo_research_lab.txt

Ask these questions in the Ask questions page:

1. What are the library's weekday opening hours according to the documents?
   Expected: The campus services guide says 8 AM–8 PM and the student handbook
   says 9 AM–7 PM. The answer should state that these sources conflict and cite
   both documents; it should not pick an authoritative schedule.

2. How often does the campus shuttle run, and where does it go?
   Expected: The services guide says every 20 minutes between the main gate and
   library. The transport notice says every 30 minutes between the main gate,
   library, and student center. State both reports as conflicting.

3. How many library books may a student borrow, and for how long?
   Expected: Up to five books for 14 days. Both the services guide and handbook
   support this; cite both when retrieved.

4. What should a passenger do to arrange step-free shuttle boarding?
   Expected: Request assistance from the campus transport desk before the trip.

5. What protective equipment and footwear are required in a wet laboratory?
   Expected: Protective eyewear and closed-toe shoes.

6. What is the student's favorite color?
   Expected: I could not find sufficient supporting information in the uploaded
   documents. This checks that the retriever abstains when evidence is weak.

7. Where is the student support desk?
   Expected: In the east wing of the administration building.

For the benchmark, upload the four demo_*.txt documents first. Their content-based
chunk IDs are seeded in evaluation_dataset.json. If you edit those files, view the
new IDs in Documents and update the editable evaluation labels.

Live answer generation requires Ollama to be running locally with the configured
policy-bot model available. Retrieval and the labeled benchmark work without Ollama.
