DocuMind Demo Collection - Research Lab Guide (Fictional, 2025)

WET-LAB SAFETY
Anyone entering a wet laboratory must wear protective eyewear. Closed-toe shoes are
also required. Laboratory staff check protective equipment before an experiment.

AFTER-HOURS ACCESS
The lab coordinator must approve after-hours access. Users record shared equipment
in the laboratory log before and after use.
